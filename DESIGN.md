# Cue interface structure

Cue is a compact broadcast console used while streaming. Its interface should feel calm, dense enough for quick operation, and predictable from one section to the next. It should resemble a purpose-built desktop tool rather than a collection of dashboard cards.

## Page types

- **Workspace** — panes for a task and its live result. Polls use a two-pane workspace. Contests use a two-pane giveaway console: setup or participant management on the left and participant or winner chat on the right.
  Polls show voting controls and matching chat messages, with no OBS preview. Poll preparation separates question and option rows from a 320px settings and launch rail at wide desktop sizes; narrow windows stack them. Active results use compact choice rows with proportion fills and visible counts; end/extend actions directly follow results. Contest eligibility remains manually editable until a winner is selected; rerolls exclude earlier winners. At narrow sizes, contest conditions lead, followed by eligibility and chat. Widget previews live in the dedicated studio section.
- Contest setup follows a three-step flow inside the primary pane: large mode choices, audience groups, then mode-specific settings. The participant pool replaces the setup flow after launch and carries search, count and the winner action; chat stays in the right pane throughout. Back and next actions keep a stable footer position. Do not expose an empty participant rail during setup or duplicate the winner action.
- **Studio** — a broadcast canvas with output actions above and contextual editing below. No permanent property sidebar. Poll, chat and contest editors share the same toolbar and OBS connection controls. Keyboard/mouse output remains out of scope.
- **Library** — search and compact kind filters above an adaptive grid of neutral bordered cards. Use and Edit stay visible; Pin and Delete live in the card menu. Drag only within one kind and pinned/unpinned group. Poll editing replaces the library with the full-width editor; other kinds open their own prepared editor. There is no secondary library rail.
- **Page** — one centered, scrolling container. Settings use this shell.
  Settings show one selected section at a time, with a 22px semibold heading above the primary scrolling content. The heading and content align within a centered 900px maximum width and 32px horizontal insets; at 540px or below the inset is 20px. Settings groups sit in quiet, theme-aware card blocks with a subtle border, 14px corners and 20px padding (16px on narrow screens). Preference-row groups keep only the horizontal padding; related rows share one block and subtle internal dividers. Blocks are 28px apart; related preference rows have a 68px minimum height, 16px vertical padding and subtle dividers. General contains Twitch identity and application toggles. Appearance, Updates, Data, Plugins, Journal and About Cue are peer sections selected from the existing app sidebar. Do not add settings search, a second sidebar, horizontal subnavigation or drilldown.
  Appearance is a radio gallery of complete theme cards: each card contains its Studio thumbnail and label on one shared surface, with 8px padding, 14px corners and a subtle border. Thumbnails use the actual theme palette tokens; the selected card has an accent border and a checkmark. Do not add a second enclosing gallery block or a repeated heading. It offers Лайм, Пичи, Лёд, Тёмная, Роза, Янтарь, Хвоя, Сапфир and Сланец. The grid has three columns, two at a settings container width of 760px or below and one at 540px or below. Each option has a 15px label; selection uses both an accent border and a checkmark, with visible keyboard focus and arrow-key navigation. Selection immediately applies the palette to the app and matching native frame and persists locally across launches. Preserve the existing `lime`, `violet` (Пичи), `ice` and `mono` identities alongside `rose`, `amber`, `forest`, `sapphire` and `slate`.
  Plugins and the error journal are ordinary settings sections, without launchers or utility windows. Plugins use a neutral divided list: Twitch, Discord and Trula have shared outline download buttons; Cue shows В разработке. Trula downloads the pinned 0.4.1 AJAZZ installer. Use the genuine Cue app asset and Trula brand SVG. Discord downloads the standalone 1.2.1 installer without an Elgato prerequisite. Installation status refreshes when the section mounts and on window focus after an installer. Keep the row layout responsive and the settings content scrollable.

The application main area never scrolls. Every page owns exactly one primary scroll surface; desktop split panes may scroll independently.

## Application shell

- Sidebar and title bar share the theme's background. The main workspace is one inset surface with a 1px subtle border, 12px radius, and 8px clearance at the right and bottom edges.
- A 40px title bar contains browser-style workspace tabs. Sidebar navigation changes the current tab; only the plus button creates a new tab. Each tab owns navigation history, visited page state and its template editor. Polls, OBS and Twitch remain shared application resources. Closing a tab never stops a running poll or contest.
- Tabs scroll horizontally when space is limited. Keep native window controls and a draggable area clear. Hiding the sidebar removes it entirely and expands content to an 8px inset; the title bar reserves room for macOS controls.
- Keep page scrolling inside the workspace frame so its corners, sidebar, and tabs stay fixed. Shell colors use theme tokens.
- All tabs are 168px wide. Labels truncate with an ellipsis; full titles remain available on hover. Tab width is independent of label length and tab count.
- Settings replace the links in the same sidebar with Общие, Оформление, Обновления, Данные, Плагины, Журнал and О Cue, in that order. Replace the bottom profile with Назад, which returns to the previous normal app route. The settings sidebar is pinned open and its collapse toggle is hidden; entering settings never changes the saved normal-app collapse preference. On exit, restore normal sidebar behavior. At browser widths of 600px or below, settings retain a pinned 64px icon rail with accessible section names. The native app has a 920px minimum window width.

## Typography

Use the native system sans stack for headings and body: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI Variable Text", "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif` (San Francisco on macOS, Segoe on Windows). Shared `text-xs` is 13px and `text-sm` is 15px. Settings section headings are 22px, weight 600; group headings are 14px, weight 500. Settings labels and plugin titles use 15px; journal titles use 16px semibold, with quieter secondary metadata. Preserve scoped sizes for dense technical details and existing specialized studio controls.

## Spatial rules

- Page inset: 24px; 32px at large widths.
- Section gap: 24px. Use 32px only between distinct page groups.
- Control gap: 8px or 12px.
- Compact surface padding: 12px or 16px.
- Form surface padding: 20px.
- Main content width: 672px inside task panes; 1152px on centered pages.
- Control rail width: 352px.
- Template cards use an adaptive grid with a 310px minimum track, bounded by the available width. Titles and descriptions wrap inside their card; the library owns its results scroll surface.
- App sidebar width: 220px visible, completely removed when hidden in normal app routes. The chosen state persists locally; settings temporarily pin the same sidebar as described above. Navigation uses 44px rows, 14px medium system labels, 18px icons with a 1.65px stroke, 10px corners and uniform 2px gaps, without additional group spacing or dividers. Selection uses a quiet foreground-tinted fill and thin border; only the selected icon uses the theme accent. On normal routes, the bottom profile sits in a 56px minimum-height, subtly bordered surface with 12px corners and the existing 28px avatar. Settings replace it with a Back button using the same shape as the navigation rows, without a divider. In the narrow settings rail, offline state is an accessibly labeled icon without wrapping text. Hover color transitions respect reduced motion.
- Surface radius: 12px for compact groups; 16px for larger panels. Journal records use 12px corners. The chat composer uses 8px corners and its emote popover uses 12px; their controls retain the shared component shapes.

## Composition

- Group fields by the decision they support. Do not wrap every field in its own card.
- Keep labels directly above their control and use the same 8px label gap.
- Put persistent actions at the end of the form or in a stable footer.
- Workspace route titles and explanatory subtitles are omitted. Settings use the selected section heading. Status belongs near the relevant control or result.
- Preview surfaces use `panel-muted`; control rails and list rails use `sidebar`. The widget studio inspector uses `surface-subtle`.
- The widget studio uses a plain OBS canvas with local dark, light, or chroma backgrounds, without a dot grid or nested safe-area frame.
- Theme primary color marks live state, selection and primary actions, including chat send. Plugins, journal actions and emote provider selection use shared neutral controls rather than scoped pastel skins. Do not use green as ambient decoration.
- Navigation is flat. The active route uses a restrained neutral fill, thin border and accented icon. The single workspace frame separates content from app chrome; individual page sections retain their existing structure.
- Section changes use a short opacity reveal without moving or scaling the page: 300ms for app routes and 260ms for settings, from fully transparent to opaque with `power1.out` easing. The wrapper does not remount cached panes or delay navigation. Skip initial renders, hidden settings and reduced motion; cancel an interrupted transition immediately.
- Metadata stays inline unless it is directly interactive. Avoid turning every value into a pill.
- Avoid nested scroll areas unless the inner area is a live feed or a reorderable list.
